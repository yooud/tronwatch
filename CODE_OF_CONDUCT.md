# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully and make technical collaboration safe and productive for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- respecting privacy and responsible disclosure;
- being clear about uncertainty, tradeoffs, and conflicts of interest;
- helping keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is violated. Enforcement decisions should consider severity, repetition, context, and the safety of affected participants.

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository owner's contact method on the hosting platform. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and reviewed without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
